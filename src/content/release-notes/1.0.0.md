## Hush is free, for good

Hush 1.0 has no trial and no license key. Everything is free and open source.

## What's new

- **Faster, more accurate models**, including streaming models that show your
  words while you speak. If your old model needs an update, Hush offers it.
- **Hold or tap.** Hold your shortcut to talk, or tap it to keep recording
  hands-free. Press Esc to cancel.
- **AI clean-up** now lives in one place and works with Groq, OpenAI,
  Anthropic, OpenRouter, Apple Intelligence or a local server. Your AI Polish
  key and app tones carried over automatically.
- **Styles per app** for any app you use, not just a fixed list.
- **Voice snippets** match however you say them ("My email." works) and work
  inside longer sentences.
- **A refreshed look** in light and dark mode.
