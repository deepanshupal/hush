## Quieter, quicker, simpler

- **Your music pauses while you dictate** and picks up again when you're done.
  Prefer something else? Choose Lower volume, Mute all sound or Do nothing in
  Settings → General → While dictating.
- **Double-tap to go hands-free.** Double-tap your shortcut to keep recording
  without holding the key, even in Hold mode. Tap again to finish.
- **Paste last transcript** with Ctrl+Cmd+V, or from the menu bar.
- **Simpler settings.** Everyday options come first; everything else lives in
  Advanced.
- **Pick a model in one click:** Fast, Live preview or Most accurate.
